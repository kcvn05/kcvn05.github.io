RootRise

UXUI & Front-End (JavaScript, React, HTML, CSS) · Figma · 2026

Middle schoolers are using AI at alarming rates and aren't using their own critical thinking skills to approach problems, especially homework.

RootRise is an education platform aimed at making learning fun and competitive through gaining XP from completing problems and teacher assigned challenges, all to meet the goal of growing the student's invidivual seedling into a fully fledged tree, as well as their team shared tree to foster friendly classroom competition.
